title: "International standardization for ECHONET Lite AIF specifications (EV Charger and EV Charger/Discharger) has been published."
date: 2026-09-02
summary: "International standardization for ECHONET Lite AIF specifications (EV Charger and EV Charger/Discharger) - ISO/IEC 14543-4-303 and ISO/IEC 14543-4-304 - has been published."
---

Kanagawa Institute of Technology, in collaboration with the ECHONET Consortium, has been proposing international standardization for the Application Communication Interface (AIF) specifications between "EV Charger" or "EV Charger/Discharger" and HEMS controller to ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 25 since FY2023, as a project commissioned by the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI). We are pleased to report that these were officially published as international standards (\*1) (\*2) on June 9th 2026.

This effort was carried out jointly by the University and members of the International Standardization Working Group under the Planning and Steering Committee of the ECHONET Consortium, with the aim of promoting the global adoption of ECHONET Lite devices.

To improve interoperability among home network devices, the ECHONET Consortium has been developing AIF specifications on top of the ECHONET Lite standard. Previously, specifications for household air conditioners (June 2020) and stationary storage batteries (April 2023) were published as international standards under METI-commissioned projects.

Following these two, international standardization efforts for EV charger and EV charger/discharger—which are expected to contribute to the effective utilization of renewable energy and home energy management—have been underway since FY2023, culminating in this current publication.

Currently, proposal activities are in progress for the AIF specifications between heat pump water heaters and HEMS controllers.
