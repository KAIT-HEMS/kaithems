Our Activities

Kanagawa Institute of Technology, Smart House Research Center is engaged in activities that promote, advance, and internationalize ECHONET Lite, a communication standard for realizing Home Energy Management Systems (HEMS) in multi-vendor environments.

As an academic member of the ECHONET Consortium, the organization responsible for developing the ECHONET Lite standard, the Center contributes through active participation in various working groups, including the Interoperability Working Group, the International Standardization Working Group, and the Web API Working Group.

The Center has established an interoperability testing environment by installing a wide range of ECHONET Lite–compliant devices within its facilities. This environment is used not only for research activities within the university but is also made available to external organizations and individuals. In particular, the facility that houses smart electric energy meters from electric power companies across Japan provides a unique testing environment that is unparalleled in the country.

The Center also develops and publishes various tools for learning the ECHONET Lite standard and for verifying the operation and interoperability of ECHONET Lite–compliant devices.
